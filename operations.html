I. Fundamental Rights: Dignity, Safety, and Freedom
• Non-Discrimination and Respect: You have the right not to be discriminated against by race, skin color, or national origin. You have the right to respect and treatment as an adult, and you have the right to personal treatment and help.
• Dignity and Safety: You have the right to privacy, dignity, and respect. You have the right to be free from any form of restraint, including chemical, physical, or mechanical restraints. You have the right to be free from abuse, neglect, intimidation, coercion, and seclusion.
• Environment: You have the right to a safe and pleasant environment with careful attention.
II. Rights to Control and Self-Determination
• Service Choice: You have the right to choose your own day care center services and activities. You have the right to direct or lead your service plan.
• Program Involvement: You have the right to participate in the organization and operation of the program.
• Personal Development: You have the right to personal interest and develop your hobby.
• Personal Control: You have control over personal finances and daily schedule. You have the right to participate with support if needed.
• Staff and Withdrawal: You have the right to request preferred staff for personal care. You have the right to stop the adult day care program at any time.
III. Rights to Communication, Privacy, and Information
• Private Space: You have access to a private space for dining, speaking on the phone, and meeting with visitors.
• Communication: You have the right to exchange and communicate with other participants or the community.
• Information Access: You have the right to receive information about your rights and to confirm consent forms related to personal information disclosure.
• Grievance: You have the right to request correction of injustice regarding discriminatory treatment.
• Notification: You have the right to be informed of any decisions that affect your care.
IV. Rights to Opportunities and Choices
• Opportunities: You have the right to seek employment or volunteer opportunities.
• Choices: You have choices of services and visitors; choices of meals and flexibility in mealtimes; choices of alternatives to services, meals, and activities; choices of community integration activities; and choices of attending appointments and social opportunities.
Acknowledgment: I have received, read, and/or had the Participant's Bill of Rights explained to me in a language I understand. I acknowledge that I understand my rights and expectations as a participant at Number One Adult Daycare.

📌 필요 (Needs)
'None' 불가 · SADC 참석 이유 및 높은 수준의 필요 사항 · 탐색 질문: "What do you hope to get out of SADC?"
🎯 목표 (Goals & Activities — SMART)

🌍 지역사회 통합 활동 (Community Integration)

💼 일/봉사 관심 (Work / Volunteer)
